4. Reklamlar, onay ve seçimleriniz

Uygulama reklam gösterir. Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı, Birleşik Krallık ve İsviçre'de, ayrıca ilgili ABD eyaletlerinde ilk açılışta Google'ın onay formu (User Messaging Platform) gösterilir. Burada kişiselleştirilmiş reklamları kabul edebilir veya reddedebilirsiniz. Reddetmeniz durumunda reklamlar kişiselleştirilmez; uygulama kullanılabilir olmaya devam eder.

Ayrıca:

  • Android Ayarlar → Gizlilik → Reklamlar bölümünden reklam kimliğinizi sıfırlayabilir veya silebilirsiniz.
  • Tek seferlik Reklamları Kaldır satın alımı banner ve geçiş reklamlarını kaldırır. Yalnızca sizin başlattığınız ödüllü reklamlar seçenek olarak kalır.

Reklam gösterimi kapsamında AdMob'un verileri iş ortaklarıyla paylaşabileceğini belirtmek isteriz; bu nedenle "verilerinizi hiçbir şekilde paylaşmıyoruz" gibi mutlak bir taahhüt verilmemektedir.

Günlük bulmaca hatırlatıcısı varsayılan olarak kapalıdır. Ayarlardan açarsanız bildirim cihazınızda yerel olarak planlanır; bunun için sunucuya veri gönderilmez. İstediğiniz zaman ayarlardan veya Android bildirim ayarlarından kapatabilirsiniz.

Seri veya istatistik kartını paylaşmayı seçtiğinizde, oluşturulan metin/görsel yalnızca Android'in paylaşım ekranına verilir ve nereye göndereceğinize siz karar verirsiniz. Uygulama bu içeriği kendi sunucularına yüklemez.

  • Cihazdaki veriler: Siz uygulamayı kaldırana veya verileri temizleyene kadar saklanır.
  • Analytics ve Crashlytics: Google'ın varsayılan saklama sürelerine tabidir (genellikle olay verisi için 14 ay, çökme raporları için 90 güne kadar).

  • Analytics/Crashlytics kayıtları: molcer111@gmail.com adresine yazın; talebiniz teknik olarak mümkün olduğu ölçüde yerine getirilir.

English

This policy explains how the Android application Kelime Ustası (com.molcerbey.kelimeustasi), also published as Word Master, Wortmeister and Maestro de Palabras, handles personal data.

In short: The app does not ask you to create an account and does not request your name, email, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, camera or microphone. Your game progress is stored on your device. Beyond that, the app uses Google services for ads, analytics and crash reporting, and those services collect their own data.

1. Controller and contact

Developer: Molcerbey
For privacy, access and deletion requests: molcer111@gmail.com

Requests are answered within a reasonable period, and no later than 30 days.

2. Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only in your device's own storage and does not leave it unless you use Play Games cloud save:

  • Level progress, completed chapters, daily and weekly puzzle results
  • Coins, lives, streaks, statistics, achievements
  • Quest, season and league state
  • Sound, haptics, theme, language and daily reminder preferences
  • Entitlement status of purchased products

You can delete this data by clearing the app's storage in system settings, or by uninstalling the app.

4. Ads, consent and your choices

The app shows ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, as well as in applicable US states, Google's User Messaging Platform consent form is shown on first launch, where you can accept or refuse personalised ads. If you refuse, ads are not personalised and the app remains fully usable.

  • You can reset or delete your advertising identifier under Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • The one-time Remove Ads purchase removes banner and interstitial ads. Rewarded ads that you start yourself remain available as an option.

Please note that AdMob may share data with its partners as part of serving ads; for this reason no absolute "we never share your data" claim is made here.

5. Notifications

The daily puzzle reminder is off by default. If you enable it, the notification is scheduled locally on your device and no data is sent to a server for this purpose. You can turn it off at any time in the app settings or Android notification settings.

6. Sharing feature

When you choose to share a streak or statistics card, the generated text or image is handed to the Android share sheet and you decide where it goes. The app does not upload this content to any server of its own.

7. Retention

  • On-device data: kept until you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Analytics and Crashlytics: subject to Google's default retention periods (typically up to 14 months for event data and up to 90 days for crash reports).
  • Cloud save: kept until you delete this game's data from your Play Games account.

8. Deleting your data

The app has no account system, so there is no profile to delete.

  • Local data: clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud save: delete this game's data in your Play Games settings.
  • Analytics/Crashlytics records: write to molcer111@gmail.com; requests are honoured as far as technically possible.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe data of a child under 13 has been processed, contact us at the address above and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

The Google services used may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Such transfers take place under Google's standard contractual clauses and safeguards.

11. Security

All communication between the app and Google services is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. No system is completely secure, but reasonable technical measures are applied.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated. For material changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change. Continuing to use the app means you accept the current policy.
